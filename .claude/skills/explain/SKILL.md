---

Explain $ARGUMENTS (a file, a function, or `diff` meaning `git diff HEAD`).

Give, briefly:
1. A 30-second version.
2. The path of one request through the code, with file:line references.
3. Why this design, and which alternative was rejected.
4. Failure modes.
5. 3 likely interviewer questions, each with a short answer.

Do not change any code.
